Constitution

📘 CONSTITUTION OF BHARAT CYBER COMMANDO

BCC — Bharat Cyber Commando

Motto: Securing India, Developing Indian Software

Adopted: 15 August 2025

Status: Internal Organizational Constitution

Nature: Unincorporated Software Development Organization

PREAMBLE

We, the members of Bharat Cyber Commando (BCC), establish this Constitution to provide an organized framework for the governance, management, development, and operation of BCC.

BCC is established as a software-focused organization dedicated to learning, developing, maintaining, and promoting responsible software and technology.

This Constitution shall serve as the supreme internal governing document of BCC, subject always to applicable law.

ARTICLE I — NAME AND IDENTITY

Section 1. Name

The organization shall be known as Bharat Cyber Commando, abbreviated as BCC.

Section 2. Motto

The official motto of BCC shall be:

“Securing India, Developing Indian Software.”

Section 3. Nature

BCC shall operate as an unincorporated software development organization/team unless and until a separate legal entity is formally established.

ARTICLE II — OBJECTS AND PURPOSES

The principal purposes of BCC shall include:

  1. Developing software, websites, applications, games, and digital tools.
  2. Promoting programming and software-development skills.
  3. Conducting responsible cybersecurity learning and development.
  4. Designing and maintaining original digital products.
  5. Encouraging innovation, teamwork, research, and technical education.
  6. Developing software intended to contribute positively to India and its digital ecosystem.

ARTICLE III — ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE

BCC shall consist of:

  1. The Founder
  2. Executive Leadership
  3. Divisions
  4. Officers and Department Heads
  5. Developers and other members
  6. Project Teams

The organizational structure may be expanded by constitutional amendment or executive establishment consistent with this Constitution.

ARTICLE IV — THE FOUNDER

Section 1. Office

The Founder shall be the person who established BCC.

Section 2. Responsibilities

The Founder shall:

  • safeguard the identity and mission of BCC;
  • provide strategic direction;
  • appoint or approve senior leadership;
  • oversee major organizational decisions;
  • protect the Constitution and core principles.

Section 3. Succession

Any permanent transfer of the Founder’s constitutional authority shall require a formal procedure established under this Constitution.

ARTICLE V — CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER

Section 1. Office

The Chief Executive Officer (CEO) shall serve as the principal executive authority responsible for the day-to-day administration and strategic execution of BCC.

Section 2. Powers

The CEO may:

  • direct organizational operations;
  • establish project teams;
  • assign responsibilities;
  • supervise development activities;
  • approve operational decisions;
  • represent BCC in appropriate organizational matters.

Section 3. Limitations

The CEO shall exercise authority in accordance with this Constitution and applicable law.

ARTICLE VI — EXECUTIVE MANAGEMENT

Executive Management shall consist of officers appointed under BCC's organizational structure.

Possible offices may include:

  • Chief Executive Officer
  • Chief Technology Officer
  • Chief Operating Officer
  • Chief Information Security Officer
  • Division Directors
  • Project Leads
  • Senior Developers

An office shall possess only those powers assigned to it by this Constitution or lawful organizational resolution.

ARTICLE VII — DIVISIONS

BCC may establish specialized divisions for specific areas of work.

Existing Divisions

1. Code Command
Responsible for software development, programming, digital design, and related development activities.

2. SCDIAS
Secure Cyber Defence & Intelligence Agency Squad, responsible for responsible cybersecurity education, defensive security projects, and authorized security research.

Division names, functions, and structures may be amended through organizational resolution.

ARTICLE VIII — MEMBERSHIP

Section 1. Eligibility

Membership shall be granted according to criteria established by BCC.

Section 2. Member Duties

Every member shall:

  • follow this Constitution;
  • perform assigned responsibilities;
  • respect other members;
  • protect confidential organizational information;
  • follow applicable laws;
  • use technology responsibly.

Section 3. Membership Status

Members may hold different positions, ranks, or responsibilities according to their role and contribution.

ARTICLE IX — APPOINTMENT AND REMOVAL

Officers and project leaders may be appointed according to the authority assigned by this Constitution.

An officer may be removed for:

  • serious violation of the Constitution;
  • misuse of organizational authority;
  • serious misconduct;
  • abandonment of assigned responsibilities;
  • other grounds established by organizational procedure.

Fair internal procedure should be followed before significant disciplinary action.

ARTICLE X — PROJECTS AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Section 1. Projects

BCC projects shall be formally identified and maintained under appropriate project records.

Section 2. Ownership

Ownership and licensing of software shall be determined according to the applicable agreement, contribution records, and law.

Section 3. Attribution

Members who make substantial contributions shall receive appropriate credit.

Section 4. Repositories

Source code may be maintained through appropriate version-control systems and repository-management procedures.

ARTICLE XI — CONFIDENTIALITY AND INFORMATION SECURITY

Members shall take reasonable measures to protect:

  • source code;
  • credentials;
  • private project information;
  • internal documents;
  • security-related information;
  • personal information.

No member shall intentionally obtain unauthorized access to systems, accounts, or information.

ARTICLE XII — CODE OF CONDUCT

All members shall conduct themselves with:

  • integrity;
  • professionalism;
  • respect;
  • responsibility;
  • technical discipline;
  • lawful and ethical behavior.

BCC shall not authorize activities intended to unlawfully compromise, damage, disrupt, or steal from computer systems.

ARTICLE XIII — MEETINGS AND RESOLUTIONS

BCC may conduct:

  • General Meetings
  • Executive Meetings
  • Division Meetings
  • Project Meetings

Major organizational decisions may be recorded through formal Resolutions.

A Resolution shall identify:

  1. the matter under consideration;
  2. the decision;
  3. the authority approving it;
  4. the date of adoption.

ARTICLE XIV — FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION

If BCC receives or generates funds, appropriate records shall be maintained.

Financial activity shall be conducted lawfully and transparently.

No member shall personally misuse organizational funds.

Any future commercial activity shall comply with applicable legal requirements.

ARTICLE XV — RECORDS AND DOCUMENTATION

BCC shall maintain organizational records where appropriate, including:

  • Constitution and amendments;
  • organizational resolutions;
  • project records;
  • member records;
  • development documentation;
  • financial records, where applicable;
  • intellectual-property records.

ARTICLE XVI — CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENTS

Section 1 — Proposal

Any proposed amendment to this Constitution shall be submitted in writing to the authorized leadership of BCC.

Section 2 — Notice of Meeting

Before an amendment is considered, an official Constitutional Amendment Meeting shall be called with reasonable notice.

Section 3 — Meeting

The proposed amendment shall be presented and discussed openly.

Section 4 — Amendment Resolution

Placed before the meeting as an Amendment Resolution.

Section 5 — Approval

Takes effect only when it receives required approval under voting provisions.

Section 6 — Recording

Recorded in the official Constitutional Amendment Register.

Section 7 — Effective Date

Takes effect immediately after adoption unless specified otherwise.

Section 8 — Publication

Updated Constitution shall be maintained as the current official version.

Section 9 — No Informal Amendment

No amendment is valid unless prescribed procedure is followed.

ARTICLE XVII — DISSOLUTION

If BCC is formally dissolved, its projects, intellectual property, digital assets, records, and other organizational property shall be handled according to applicable agreements and law.

Any outstanding obligations shall be addressed before final closure.

ARTICLE XVIII — TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

Until additional officers, divisions, procedures, and members are formally established, existing leadership may temporarily perform functions necessary for the continued operation of BCC, subject to this Constitution.

ARTICLE XIX — SUPREMACY OF THE CONSTITUTION

This Constitution shall constitute the highest internal governing document of BCC. All internal policies, regulations, resolutions, and organizational procedures shall be consistent with it.

ARTICLE XX — ADOPTION

This Constitution is hereby adopted as the foundational internal governing document of Bharat Cyber Commando (BCC).

Our Direction

Manifesto

📜 BCC MANIFESTO

1. LEARN — Knowledge First

BCC believes that learning is the foundation of technology. Members shall continuously improve their knowledge and skills.

2. CODE — Turn Knowledge Into Technology

BCC shall transform knowledge and ideas into useful software, websites, applications, games, and digital tools.

3. BUILD — Create Useful Projects

BCC shall encourage members to build practical projects that solve problems and provide useful digital solutions.

4. Responsible Cybersecurity

BCC shall promote responsible, defensive, ethical, and lawful cybersecurity learning and research.

5. Indian Software Development

BCC shall encourage the development of original software and digital technology that can contribute positively to India's digital ecosystem.

6. Innovation and Research

BCC shall encourage experimentation, research, creative ideas, and the development of new technological solutions.

7. Teamwork and Respect

BCC members shall work together with discipline, respect, cooperation, and responsibility.

8. Quality and Continuous Improvement

BCC shall aim to improve its software, projects, systems, documentation, and technical standards continuously.

9. Ethical and Lawful Technology

BCC technology shall be developed and used responsibly, ethically, safely, and according to applicable law.

10. Build the Future Through Technology

BCC shall seek to develop future-ready technology through learning, coding, building, teamwork, innovation, and responsible development.

🤝 OATH OF BHARAT CYBER COMMANDO

“I solemnly pledge that, as a member of Bharat Cyber Commando, I shall uphold the Constitution, values, and principles of BCC.

I shall use my knowledge of software and technology responsibly, ethically, and lawfully.

I shall respect my fellow members, protect the work and information entrusted to me, and carry out my responsibilities with honesty, discipline, and dedication.

I shall learn, code, build, and continuously improve my skills for the development of useful and responsible software.

I shall protect the integrity and reputation of BCC and never knowingly misuse its name, resources, or technology.

With knowledge as my strength and responsibility as my duty, I pledge to serve the mission of BCC.”
